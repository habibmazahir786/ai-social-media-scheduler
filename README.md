## How AI fits into scheduling

An AI social media scheduler combines a publishing calendar or queue with AI-assisted tasks such as drafting variations, organizing ideas, adapting copy for different channels, or suggesting a posting plan. The exact capabilities vary by product. AI can accelerate the work, but it does not know your audience, verify every claim, or take responsibility for a post. Treat generated material as a draft: check facts, rights, brand voice, accessibility, and platform requirements before approving it.

Traditional schedulers primarily let a person prepare posts and set publishing dates. AI-powered social media scheduling may add recommendations and content assistance around that same calendar. Neither approach guarantees reach or engagement; performance depends on the audience, content, context, and platform.

## Use the evaluation framework

When comparing social media scheduling tools, score each candidate against the same criteria:

1. **Channel fit:** Does it support the accounts and post formats you actually use?
2. **Publishing rules:** Can it publish the formats you need, or does a workflow require a reminder and manual completion?
3. **Planning:** Can you review a calendar, collaborate, and manage drafts and approvals?
4. **AI controls:** Can you review and edit suggestions before they are scheduled? Are generated claims and media rights addressed?
5. **Accountability:** Are roles, approvals, audit history, and failure notifications appropriate for your team?
6. **Privacy and security:** Are access scopes, retention, data use, and account disconnection explained?
7. **Usability and cost:** Does the time saved justify the price and operational overhead?

See the complete [platform comparison framework](docs/choosing-an-ai-social-media-scheduler.md) and [tools and platform references](resources/tools-and-platforms.md).

## Suggested starting workflow

1. Set a goal, audience, and a manageable publishing cadence.
2. Capture ideas in the [content planning template](resources/content-planning-template.md).
3. Draft and adapt posts for each intended channel; use AI only where it helps.
4. Verify details, links, rights, accessibility, and platform constraints.
5. Schedule approved posts in the appropriate timezone.
6. Review publishing status and outcomes, then update the next plan.

The [scheduling workflow](resources/scheduling-workflow.md) expands these steps into a repeatable checklist.
