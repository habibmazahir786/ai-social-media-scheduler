# Review-First Scheduling Workflow

Use this checklist for a weekly planning cycle or a short campaign. Adapt the approvals and checks to the account, platform, audience, and organization. The workflow works with a traditional scheduler or an AI-powered social media scheduler.

## Before drafting

- [ ] Define the audience, goal, campaign dates, and desired action.
- [ ] Confirm the person responsible for the content and the person authorized to approve it.
- [ ] Gather verified facts, destination links, brand guidance, and approved media.
- [ ] Identify channel-specific formats, account requirements, and timezones.
- [ ] Decide what information may be shared with any AI service.
- [ ] Record the plan in the [content planning template](content-planning-template.md).

## Prepare channel versions

- [ ] Draft from the approved brief, whether written by a person or AI-assisted.
- [ ] Adapt the copy and media to each channel rather than assuming one version fits all.
- [ ] Check names, dates, figures, claims, offer terms, and URLs against the source material.
- [ ] Confirm rights, permissions, consent, and any required disclosures.
- [ ] Check accessible media details, including alternative text where the destination supports it and captions for relevant video.
- [ ] Preview the final content for the intended account and format.

## Approve and schedule

- [ ] Obtain and record approval for each version.
- [ ] Select the correct channel and account.
- [ ] Confirm the date, time, and timezone shown by the scheduler.
- [ ] Check that the selected format can publish automatically; note any required manual step.
- [ ] Verify the scheduled item appears once in the calendar or queue.
- [ ] Keep the owner and an escalation contact clear for the scheduled period.

## Monitor and close the loop

- [ ] Check publishing status after the scheduled time.
- [ ] If status is unclear, inspect the destination account before retrying to avoid duplicate posts.
- [ ] Route failures, rejected posts, and unexpected changes to the responsible person.
- [ ] Pause or update affected queued content when facts, offers, or circumstances change.
- [ ] Review outcomes against the original goal, including time spent and corrections made.
- [ ] Record one or two improvements for the next planning cycle.

For platform selection and test criteria, see the [tool comparison framework](../docs/choosing-an-ai-social-media-scheduler.md). For the distinction between automation and AI, read the [automation guide](../docs/social-media-automation-guide.md).
